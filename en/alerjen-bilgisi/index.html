Allergens

Allergen information
Under every item on our menu the allergens it contains are shown as codes. Our kitchen handles gluten, milk, eggs, peanuts, tree nuts, sesame, celery and fish, so cross-contact may occur. If in doubt, please ask our staff.
menu_bookHow to read the menu
GLU gluten, MLK milk, EGG eggs, PNT peanuts, CEL celery, SES sesame, FSH fish.
